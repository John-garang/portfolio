CNN Academy

CNN Academy Voices of the Global South Climate Storytelling Fellowship

July – November 2025 Global (Online)

A competitive international journalism fellowship supporting emerging storytellers from the Global South in producing impactful, human-centered climate narratives. I completed the CNN Academy Learning Hub curriculum covering journalism ethics, OSINT, mobile journalism, LumaFusion editing, AI in journalism, and newsroom workflows.

For my final pitch, I developed a story on how South Sudanese and Global South writers use poetry and creative spaces for climate advocacy. Though not selected for the Abu Dhabi newsroom simulation, the process deepened my climate storytelling skills. I now hold lifetime access to the CNN Academy Learning Hub.

Climate Journalism Multimedia Storytelling Digital Communications OSINT Mobile Journalism Global South Climate Advocacy
"The most powerful climate stories are not only about the environment, but about the people using their voices to protect its future."

UNLEASH

UNLEASH Global Innovation Lab

December 2–9, 2023 Kigali, Rwanda

Selected on a full scholarship as one of ~1,000 global innovators, I participated in the UNLEASH Global Innovation Lab in Kigali — one of the world's leading platforms for youth-led, SDG-aligned innovation. Working with teammates from The Gambia, Chile, the UK, Pakistan, and Cambodia, our team created PappyBags — biodegradable bags made from South Sudan's papyrus reeds as an alternative to single-use plastics. Our solution advanced to the knockout stage before the finals.

Sustainable Development Goals Climate Innovation Entrepreneurship Design Thinking African Leadership Innovation
"Innovation begins by seeing opportunity where everyone else sees ordinary."

Tilting Futures (formerly Global Citizen Year)

Take Action Lab

March – June 2024 Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa

A full-scholarship international leadership program — cohort of 52 leaders from 37 countries. I served as Multimedia Apprentice at the Surplus People Project (SPP), a South African social justice organisation advancing food sovereignty, agroecology, and land rights. I documented grassroots work across the Western, Northern, Eastern Cape, and Gauteng provinces through photography, videography, and strategic communications.

Field work took me to Mamre, Wolseley, Springbok, Loeriesfontein, Namakwa Vanrhynsdorp, Boontjiesrivier, Johannesburg, Pretoria, and Paarl. I facilitated a three-day social media training for SAFE Youth and covered the National Indaba Conference for Tshintsha Amakhaya.

Human Rights Agroecology Food Sovereignty Strategic Communications Community Development Global Leadership Gauteng South Africa
"The most powerful stories are those told alongside the communities living them."

U.S. Department of State · RELO · TUSSAA

Eastern Africa English for Business & Entrepreneurship Hackathon (E4BE Hackathon)

February – May 2026 Arusha, Tanzania 14 Countries

A U.S. Department of State–funded regional initiative for young African changemakers across 14 countries, focused on business communication and market-driven innovation. I completed the MOOC phase and advanced as one of the top 90 regional finalists to compete in Arusha.

My team developed MedLink — a low-bandwidth, "crisis-first" teleconsultation app bridging stranded patients and displaced doctors in conflict zones like Sudan, using a tiered pricing model ($2–$15) and a 25% gross margin structure for sustainability.

Healthcare Technology Crisis Innovation Strategy Cross-Border Collaboration Pitching & Venture Capital Business Communication Lean Business Modeling
"Every great venture starts by turning a modern connection into a lifeline for those who need it most."

Google Africa Headquarters · African Leadership University (ALU)

Nairobi Nexus Immersive Hackathon

June 2025 Nairobi, Kenya Innovation Team Lead & Presenter

As part of ALU's Nairobi Nexus immersion, I participated in a 90-minute hackathon at Google Africa HQ, pitching to industry tech leaders. As Innovation Team Lead, I drove problem identification, UX mapping, wireframing, and pitch architecture using Google Firebase.

My team built SimuSalama — a fraud-prevention web app letting users report suspicious mobile money transactions, cross-reference a crowdsourced fraud database, and receive real-time risk scores. I delivered the opening pitch and navigated a mid-presentation disruption, coordinating a seamless transition to the live demo.

Rapid Prototyping Product Strategy & UX Agile Team Leadership Crisis Communication FinTech Security Solutions Entrepreneurial Leadership
"Every great venture starts by turning a high-pressure challenge into an opportunity to build real-world resilience."
