Programs & Fellowships

A curated journey through global innovation, leadership development, and transformative learning experiences

CNN Academy Fellow

July 2025 – Nov 2025  ·  Global (Online)

An immersive journalism fellowship sharpening editorial skills through newsroom-style learning, masterclasses with CNN correspondents, and peer collaboration focused on climate storytelling from a Global South perspective.

"The program challenged us to think critically about representation, ensuring that stories from our regions are told with accuracy, dignity, and urgency."

UNLEASH Global Innovation Lab

December 2–9, 2023  ·  Kigali, Rwanda

Selected as one of 1,000 young talents worldwide to co-create scalable solutions for the UN Sustainable Development Goals — developing PappyBags, a sustainable alternative to single-use plastics using papyrus reeds.

"The experience deepened my commitment to applying creativity and local resources toward climate resilience."

Take Action Lab

Sept – Dec 2024  ·  East Africa

An immersive leadership program equipping young African leaders with frameworks for systemic change — intensive workshops on systems thinking, community organizing, and social entrepreneurship.

"Real change starts with understanding the systems that shape our communities and having the courage to challenge them."

Accra Fusion

2024  ·  Accra, Ghana

A dynamic program bringing together young African leaders, entrepreneurs, and innovators to foster cross-cultural understanding. Built lasting partnerships with changemakers across the continent.

"Africa's future is being shaped by young people who refuse to accept limitations and dare to reimagine what's possible."

YALI East Africa

2024  ·  East Africa Region

A signature Young African Leaders Initiative empowering young leaders through intensive training, mentorship, and networking across Business & Entrepreneurship, Public Management, and Civic Leadership tracks.

"Leadership is not about titles or positions — it's about taking responsibility for the future you want to see."
