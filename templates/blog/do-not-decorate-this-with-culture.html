Do Not Decorate This With Culture

John GarangFebruary 16, 2026 · 3 min read

First of all, this is not just disgusting; it is a daylight exhibition of moral and social decay and collapse. And it reveals a lot — more than small chicken brains can think.

This child was unfortunately not failed by one man. She is surrounded by greedy brothers, uncles, and a father whose spines dissolved the moment cows were mentioned. Her mother has probably learned to swallow words to survive, that's if she's alive to witness this unfold. And if she is, then she has failed to understand that silence is not living, because the only life permitted to be quiet is one inside a cemetery.

Chieftaincy is mostly a position given out of privilege, not out of utmost good faith, because nothing signals wisdom from this fossil. And he's very lucky not to have an in-law like me, because I would cut his dying thing off permanently and send him to hell so he can curse me with his ancestors.

Please! Please, do not decorate this with culture. This is not the culture we defend left and right. This is not the loor Jieng I'll drop anything to go dance to. Culture does not erect at the sight of a child. Like, tell me how? How does someone even erect to that child that I cannot even view as a lady in any way? Any sexual thought around that child alone is a disgusting act and a disgrace to human conscience. A chief looking at that child and feeling sexual desire isn't just confused; he is rotten and a disgrace to his chieftaincy.

Culturally, many people have always felt uncomfortable with having their sisters, friends, nieces, etc., married off in such a disgusting way. Long ago, dissent was silenced by rumors of curses. Elders could threaten spiritual thunder. But the people then were foolish enough, and somehow, even though times have tried to change things, foolishness has surprisingly been growing in some areas. Today? There is nothing mystical about showing old presumed-wise men how foolish and disgusting they are. No ancestor signed this madness, and if they did, they were never human to begin with — and I believe humanity is a precedent to being an ancestor, for one needs to live to be one. No god blesses a deal sealed with a child's future.

There are grown women all over this country, yet he bypassed all of them and forcefully got a girl who has barely understood her own body. And somewhere, some fool is happy because his uncle has married, and some brother is counting cows with his fingers like they are medals, and some uncle is planning which stupid thing he will buy.

Damn this society!

You will rant on Facebook. You will cry in comment sections. Feminists will use this opportunity to climb up the ladder, not to erect change but to summon influence like prayer mats after dawn. But when it is time to confront the father, the chief, the uncle — we become experts in rhetorical minimalism. Hypocrisy is why I struggle with activism.

Rage, however, is honest. Rage is the last evidence that something inside us is still alive. The first comment will be from a potential pedophile, coming to defend this using culture. He or she will call it culture, tradition, agreement. Nothing justifies this, and if this was my old man, I would send him to the grave to grow him some sense there. But hey, this is just a dissenting voice in a rotten society.

Dear child, you never chose to be born into greed disguised as family looking out for your best interests. The only choice of family you ever have in life is where you will age, yet here it is already decided for you like your birth was. They took the one thing that would truly define the course of your life.

May the God of kids — if justice still breathes — hold you, avenge for you. May fate remember you. And may this rotten society one day choke on the very culture it uses to defend its crimes. If this sounds loud, then maybe the problem was never the noise. Someday fate will lead us to a deserved destiny, and some of you deserve to experience the worst of human rights violations to feel just how the weak and voiceless should be defended, not exploited.

There's no ground to justify this, but you all support SPLM and you are retarded. Your kids will be ashamed of parents like you!
