Dear Jieng: The Wife Is Not the Enemy

John GarangJune 1, 2026 · 5 min read

Dear Jieng,

We need to stop blaming women for the alleged "stinginess" of their husbands.

Actually, no.

We need to stop expecting other people's sweat to finance our laziness, because no one should become another person's paycheck just because you share blood.

Our community suffers from a social disease that needs critical thought. When a man decides to marry and inherits new responsibilities, the siblings, cousins, parents, uncles, aunts, and even neighbors all adopt a new theory. Not one explaining the rampant unemployment, not even one explaining why a healthy 35-year-old man is surviving off his younger cousin's paycheck.

No.

The theory is always: "His wife has changed him. She now wants all that money for herself and her family." The poor woman who arrived weeks ago with her one suitcase is now the alleged ultimate supreme being with the supernatural ability to mind-control a grown man. Before their marriage, this philanthropist was the family's local branch of the World Bank and offered unsolicited, no-strings grants. After the marriage, this one-man humanitarian organization had to close down because he had to assume his responsibilities, and this choice to stop being a relief service provider is where the accusations of witchcraft against the wife arise.

Iwee 🫩😂 Even God, at some point, stopped sending manna from heaven and told people to work the land. Now, did this man create the world? 🫩 Go and read your Bible well. The "I, the Lord, will provide for you all the days of your life" verse was not written for or by this guy!

There is no place where free food is a constitutional right. Because why is a 34-year-old man with functioning legs, functioning arms, and a sane head sitting under a tree hating on an 18-year-old bride because his monthly allowance from a 24-year-old cousin or brother has disappeared?

Think about this carefully. You are a grown man. If they call you a boy, you almost want to kill people, but you're here competing with a teenage girl over the resources of her husband? It's funny how the gratitude expires when this bachelor who has been helping you gets married. Now that he has decided you are not his spouse and your wife and your lavish lifestyle are not his responsibility, all of a sudden he is arrogant. He is not a good man. Budgeting is now control, investing is bewitchment, and prioritizing his children is forgetting his people.

Muonyjang, what exactly do we need? For a man to remain poor forever while he funds our lifestyles? No one man is a mango tree that the ancestors planted long ago to provide shade endlessly and for the entire village to harvest from until it dries up.

A man is a vessel, and as he sails through life, changes occur. He adapts to new responsibilities and has to abandon some, especially those to which he did not directly contribute. Yes, he can help you because you share blood, but he didn't sire your kids. He has no covenant with your wife to provide for her. He has no obligation to send your kids to school. But his wife? He took an oath to take care of her. His children? He brought them into this world, and he's not about to give them a substandard life just so you can call him a good person while you spend your day playing dominoes.

Marriage in the real world is not like the telenovelas you see on TV. It is an economic institution. The moment a man marries, his calculations shift from Friday nights to the next 20 years. A married man has to think about rent, school fees, medical bills, emergencies, his wife, future children, elderly parents, unexpected funerals, inflation, and the fact that death does not send appointment letters before arriving.

A married man has to swallow maturity like a bitter herb, not because it's sweet, but because if he doesn't, he'll become useless when he ceases to have anything going on for himself.

Here's the plain truth: women are expensive, not because they always demand it, but because a man is a proud creature and voluntarily wants the best for his wife. He wants her to look presentable because she is a reflection of his financial ability, and even poor men want to project wealth. And this is a societal pressure, because when women tell themselves, "We will know your man by your hair," this is a challenge for the husband. He will work to see his wife comfortable, healthy, and respected. Not every man should be like you, who envies other men's well-kept families while his own household looks like a post-cyclone street. He wants his children to feel proud of his presence. He wants them dressed in clothes they desire, labels, and new trends in town. And then there's you. You also want to buy labeled clothes from another man's sweat? Are you his wife? Is he your husband? Are you his child? Is he your father?

A man has to show he's a man. And that is the first-degree responsibility he has, not to anyone else but to the family he creates. It's called responsibility. Just because your house looks like an abandoned construction project doesn't mean he should magically come and change it. No man should neglect his immediate responsibilities to finance yours.

Such an economic model doesn't exist.

Why are you even getting angry that another man's wife and child are living the life you want? Why is the problem always the wife? The real problem is that man in your mirror every morning when you brush your teeth, eat breakfast, and go back to bed again.

Dependency is a scam because it doesn't always come out of its shell. It disguises itself as family support, community, and solidarity. But to be honest, dependency is a nice way of unveiling laziness wearing traditional clothes.

Sponsorships expire. Don't become so comfortable living in somebody else's pocket. At some point, even parents cut support when their kids turn 25, but you want to be supported by someone until death do you part?

Stop hating innocent people. The wife is not the enemy. The children are not. The marriage is not the issue. The real enemy is the unemployed man staring back at you in the mirror.

Instead of pointing fingers, think about your growth. Ask yourself, "How do I make my own money?" "How do I become independent?" "How do I build something for my children?" Don't be a retard who wakes up every morning asking, "Why is another man no longer feeding me?"

Bro, you are not one of his wives. You are not one of his children. You are not one of his responsibilities. You are a fully grown adult.

Please behave accordingly.

Respectfully, © John Garang
