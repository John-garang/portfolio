Build With Someone Your Age

John GarangMay 28, 2026 · 3 min read

I believe in building together with someone within your age range, so listen, ladies.

Going for an already established guy in his early 40s might seem like the perfect escape plan, but you could end up trading something more valuable, your happiness, for a seat at a dining table where three or four other women are already sitting, or will be joining soon. A huge age gap comes with different levels of maturity, and he'll expect you, at 22, to behave like someone in their 40s.

That's just not realistic. At 22, you're still young. You wanna be loved loudly and intentionally. You deserve random ice cream dates, dropping the kids at grandma's place and staying out till midnight with your man. But that shit rarely happens in a 20-year age gap relationship. And the moment you cross a small line because you're still figuring life out, he barks, maybe even starts hitting you, and because you came for the financial stability, you end up tolerating abuse and calling it "behaving," as if living your age is somehow misbehaving.

But yeah, you can't have your cake and eat it too.

Now to my original point. When I say build with someone your age, I mean understanding that most people don't even reach their financial peak until 35+, and many millionaires don't become millionaires until their 40s or 50s. People think success happens overnight. It doesn't. And honestly, you wanna be part of the story, not arrive at page 89. You wanna be there from the prologue.

Some things just need common sense. Nobody should have to tell you to build with someone who has vision. Lady, use your head. And when we say build with someone your age, we don't mean go waste your years on some bum. Nah. Don't build with a dreamer who never gets out of bed, or some street niqqa. That niqqa in the studio trying to become a rapper? Fuck him. What exactly are you building with that dude? Truth is, his chances are probably as slim as me becoming the president of SSD.

There's nothing wrong with being a rapper, but success in the music industry is rare, and even rarer in hip-hop or dancehall. At least some local artists are surviving off diaspora fans and whatever money gets sent back home, but tell me one rapper who has truly made it in SSD.

There's a huge difference between starting life with a guy who is already in law school and doing well, versus building with someone who merely dreams about going to law school. One is potential. The other is just possibility.

Here's the thing: you need strong discernment. You need to know who is actually worth building with, and you also need to be ready, able, and willing to leave if necessary. But don't say "I'm building with my man" when all you do is sit at home and add nothing to either his life or yours.

Get your shit together and have something going for yourself too. Because when all you bring to the table is yourself, they'll leave. And honestly, it would be stupid to expect him to stay if your only contribution is saying, "I helped build." Oh, so just showing up was the cement in his foundation?

Be the kind of woman he proudly introduces because your name is respected in places that have nothing to do with him, because of the shit you've built for yourself.

At the end of the day, a life you helped build can never truly be taken away from you, same with the man you built with. And don't let bitter people project their failed relationships onto you with all that "men always leave" talk and "better to cry in a Lamborghini" nonsense. That's their pain speaking. You haven't done evil, so trust God to make things work in your favor.

Don't let someone else's failed relationship manifest fear into your life. Go build with that man. And if he's just rapping or bombo clatting around with no direction, leave his bare ass.

Thank you for your attention to this matter!
