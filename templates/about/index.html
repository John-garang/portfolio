About John Ngor Deng Garang

John Ngor Deng Garang is a South Sudanese communications professional, graphic designer, and social impact leader specializing in regional development and community storytelling. He has managed corporate and non-profit communications initiatives across multiple African nations, including South Africa, Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya, and Ghana. He served as the Communications Lead for Education Bridge until September 2026 and currently works as Executive Office Assistant in the Dean's Office of the Paris School of International Affairs (PSIA) at Sciences Po and as Social Media Manager for UrbanGeekz. Recognized globally for his leadership and media acumen, Garang is a CNN Academy Fellow, a UNLEASH Innovation Lab Talent, and a Young African Leaders Initiative (YALI) alumnus. He holds a Bachelor of Science from the African Leadership University and is a Mastercard Foundation Scholar pursuing a Master's degree at Sciences Po Paris.

01

Current Focus

John is currently pursuing a Master of Communications, Media, and Creative Industries at Sciences Po France School of Management and Impact, in Paris. He applies his skills in communication, strategy, and engagement to create meaningful impact. Whether managing projects, writing compelling narratives, or leading partnerships, his work consistently stands out and strengthens organizations.

Throughout his career, John has developed close to 1000 content pieces, managed social media platforms that saw rapid growth, expanded the online presence of organizations, and facilitated key partnerships through strategic communication. He has also facilitated learning experiences, coordinated projects, and built meaningful partnerships. His greatest strength lies in communication and engagement; understanding people, bridging gaps, and ensuring messages resonate.

He has participated in the UNLEASH Global Innovation Lab as talent, the Young African Leaders Initiative (YALI), the UN Conference of Youth on Climate Change as a delegate, the Take Action Lab, and the Jim Leech Mastercard Foundation on Entrepreneurship. Each of these experiences has refined his ability to lead, innovate, and create lasting change.
