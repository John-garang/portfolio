The Not-a-Tourist Price

John GarangFlorence and Vatican City · November 4, 2026 · 1 min read

You don't feel so insulted when there's something to gain. 😅🤦🏽‍♂️

So, I went to a store with Maghai Abul-dit in Florence (Firenze) to buy a souvenir. The souvenir was priced at €20, and then this Bangladeshi guy who was the shopkeeper said, "This, not your price. This American and tourist price. You, we give discount."

He eventually dropped the price from €20 to €10, and then back up to €12 because I didn't have cash and had to pay with a card.

Like, niqqa, what do you mean I am not a tourist? I travel all the way from France to Italy for a tour, and somehow you think I am not a tourist? 🫩 Probably because I'm black, you think I am just another guy from a desperate journey. 🥱

I also faced the same thing in Vatican City, where the price of a souvenir was reduced from €8 to €5 by a Moroccan trader.

But I didn't take it as an insult then, because I was getting a discount for my assumed economic status. Of course, I am a poor niqqa, but how dare you assume that without checking how much I have in my accounts? 😅😅😅
