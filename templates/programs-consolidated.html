CNN Academy Fellow

About CNN Academy

CNN Academy equips the next generation of journalists with real-world skills through immersive, newsroom-style learning. Drawing on CNN's legacy, it brings newsroom expertise into classrooms—from Dublin to Kuala Lumpur—connecting students with top CNN correspondents and transforming storytelling into action.

Fellowship Experience

As a CNN Academy Global South Fellow, I spent months immersed in an intensive program designed to sharpen both my technical and editorial skills. The fellowship was a unique blend of online courses, expert-led masterclasses, and peer-to-peer collaboration, all focused on harnessing storytelling to address climate change from a Global South perspective.

"The program challenged us to think critically about representation, ensuring that stories from our regions are told with accuracy, dignity, and urgency."

UNLEASH Global Innovation Lab

Global Talent | Climate and Land Track

December 2-9, 2023 | Kigali, Rwanda

About UNLEASH

UNLEASH is a nonprofit powerhouse on a mission to accelerate progress toward the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by mobilizing young changemakers from around the world. They gather top talents (ages 18–35) for immersive Innovation Labs where diverse youth co-create scalable, real-world solutions.

Kigali Lab Experience

I joined as one of 1,000 young talents selected from around the world, placed in the Climate and Land track. I collaborated with a team from Colombia, The Gambia, USA, Pakistan, and Iceland to develop PappyBags—a sustainable solution replacing single-use plastic bags in South Sudan using locally abundant papyrus reeds.

"We progressed to the first knockout stage, and the experience deepened my commitment to applying creativity and local resources toward climate resilience."

The Young African Leaders Initiative (YALI) East Africa is a signature program empowering young African leaders through leadership training, mentorship, and networking opportunities. YALI focuses on three key tracks: Business & Entrepreneurship, Public Management, and Civic Leadership.
