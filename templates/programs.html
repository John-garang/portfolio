CNN Academy Fellow

Voices of the Global South

July 2025 - November 2025

About the Program

CNN Academy equips the next generation of journalists with real-world skills through immersive, newsroom-style learning. Drawing on CNN's legacy, it brings newsroom expertise into classrooms—connecting students with top CNN correspondents and transforming storytelling into action.

My Experience

As a CNN Academy Global South Fellow, I spent months immersed in an intensive program designed to sharpen both technical and editorial skills. The fellowship blended online courses, expert-led masterclasses, and peer collaboration, all focused on harnessing storytelling to address climate change from a Global South perspective.

"The program challenged us to think critically about representation, ensuring that stories from our regions are told with accuracy, dignity, and urgency."

UNLEASH Global Innovation Lab

Global Talent | Climate and Land Track

December 2-9, 2023 | Kigali, Rwanda

About UNLEASH

UNLEASH is a nonprofit accelerating progress toward the UN Sustainable Development Goals by mobilizing young changemakers. They gather top talents (ages 18–35) for immersive Innovation Labs where diverse youth co-create scalable, real-world solutions.

Kigali Lab Experience

I joined as one of 1,000 young talents selected globally, placed in the Climate and Land track. I collaborated with a team from Colombia, The Gambia, USA, Pakistan, and Iceland to develop PappyBags—a sustainable solution replacing single-use plastic bags in South Sudan using locally abundant papyrus reeds.

"We progressed to the first knockout stage, and the experience deepened my commitment to applying creativity and local resources toward climate resilience."
